# Name options

Criteria:
- Short, and easy to say from a stage.
- Hints at "many people moving as one, sensed live".
- Works across all personas.
- Doesn't clash with a big cloud-native project.

Before locking a name, do a 2-minute check: GitHub repo name, npm, and a web search.

## Shortlist (recommended)
| Name | Why it fits | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **Murmuration** | A flock of starlings moving as one, with no central control. It's exactly crowd flow as emergent telemetry, and it's beautiful on a title slide. "Murmur" also hints at the backchannel | Long. Use "Murmur" as the short form |
| **Muster** | To assemble people, and a ship's roll call. Fits Kubernetes' nautical naming (helm, harbor, kubernetes = helmsman). Punchy | Small existing apps use the name |
| **Sonder** | The realization that every passer-by has a life as vivid as yours. The inclusion story in one word | A hotel brand exists. Fine for a hackathon |
| **Plenary** | The session where everyone is present. Conference-native, and sounds like infrastructure | A bit formal |

## Creative / metaphorical
- **Undercurrent**: what's really happening beneath the talk.
- **Foyer**: where conferences actually happen.
- **Mezzanine**: the level between stage and crowd.
- **Wayfinder**: guidance for every persona.
- **Polyphony**: many voices, one piece.
- **Tessera**: one tile of a mosaic. Every attendee is a data point.
- **Commons**: shared space, inclusive.
- **Lanyard**: the one object every persona wears.
- **Encore**: catch-me-up and the post-talk digest.
- **Shoal**: fish moving together. A lighter Murmuration.
- **Quorum**: enough people present to decide. A nod to distributed systems (Raft/etcd).

## Infra-flavoured (plays to the "conference as production" pitch)
- **Kubecrowd**: too on the nose, but memorable at KubeCon.
- **Heartbeat**: liveness of the event.
- **Pulse**: generic but instantly understood.
- **Telemetry for Humans**: works as a tagline, not a name.

## Taglines that pair with any name
- "Run your conference like production."
- "Every person in the building, one live event graph."
- "Conferences, observable."
